WHO WE ARE

Built on precision.

SR Engineering Design Solutions is a Hyderabad-based engineering and BIM team focused on accurate, coordinated and construction-ready project deliverables.

With a dedicated team of 23–35 skilled BIM professionals, we provide end-to-end services across BIM Modeling, BIM Coordination, Clash Detection, MEP Design & Drafting, Shop Drawings and Construction Documentation.

Our collaborative approach combines technical expertise, digital workflows and practical project understanding to help teams deliver with confidence.
